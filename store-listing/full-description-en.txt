THE GARDEN THAT GROWS WITH YOU
Plant seeds at the start of each session. Work through your timer and the plant grows, stage by stage, into a full-grown pixel art flower or tree. Give up early? The plant withers. Your garden is a living record of your focus streaks — keep your plants healthy by finishing what you start.

Collect different plant species as you hit milestones and build your very own pixel art garden over time.

A TIMER YOU CAN ACTUALLY TRUST
Most browser-based timers slow down when your screen is off or the app moves to the background. Focus Valley uses a background-safe timer that keeps ticking accurately even when the app is minimized — so your 25-minute session is always 25 minutes, no matter what.
